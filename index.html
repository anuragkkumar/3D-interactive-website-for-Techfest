Flagship Arenas & Competition Dossiers

Official challenge categories and engineering specifications for Techfest 2026. All shortlisted zonal and online teams qualify for the national finals at the IIT Bombay campus.

⚔️ International Robowars ₹12 LAKH

Heavyweight combat robotics inside a 10m x 10m x 4m bulletproof polycarbonate cage. Bots battle with active spinners, drums, and pneumatic flippers.

Weight Classes: 60kg (Heavy) & 15kg (Feather)
Match Duration: 3 Minutes Combat
Arena Hazards: Spike Strips & Flame Pits

💻 National 36-Hour Hackathon ₹10 LAKH

Non-stop prototype sprint. Engineering teams build production-grade web applications, generative AI workflows, and decentralized protocols under senior tech mentors.

Tracks: AI/ML, Web3, FinTech & Cloud
Incentives: AWS & NVIDIA GPU Credits
Recruitment: Direct Sponsor Company PPOs

🤖 Meshmerize Autonomous ₹4.5 LAKH

Ultra-responsive autonomous robots navigating dynamic maze layouts and shifting grid tracks using onboard PID controllers and IR sensor arrays.

Track Dimensions: 15m x 12m Dynamic Grid
Max Bot Size: 250mm x 250mm x 250mm
Control Mode: 100% Autonomous (No RF)

🛸 Drone Prix Obstacle Cup ₹8 LAKH

High-velocity FPV drone racing through illuminated multi-tier aerial rings, slalom gates, and tunnel hazards at speeds reaching 120 km/h.

Frame Wheelbase: Max 250mm FPV Quad
Video Link: 5.8 GHz Analog / Digital HD
Safety Protocol: Fail-Safe Disarm Killswitch

🧠 AI & Quantum Track ₹6.5 LAKH

Build enterprise computer vision, real-time edge speech models, and optimization algorithms for automated industrial environments.

Hardware: NVIDIA Jetson / Cloud Tensor
Evaluation: Latency, F1 Score & Accuracy
Mentorship: 1-on-1 Senior AI Engineers

🦾 Cozmo Clench Robotics ₹4 LAKH

Precision pick-and-place gripper robotics maneuvering complex obstacle courses and delivering fragile payloads against a strict chronometer.

Actuators: Servo & Stepper Grippers
Track Layout: Bridge, Ramp & Pit Zones
Drive Train: Omni / Differential Steering
