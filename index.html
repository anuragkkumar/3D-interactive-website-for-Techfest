Getting to IIT Bombay

Airport: Mumbai Airport (BOM) Terminal 2 is 8 km away (~25 mins via JVLR).
Train: Kanjurmarg Station (Central Line) is 3 km away. Auto-rickshaws reach Main Gate in 10 minutes.
Entry: Main Gate & Y-Point Gate are 24/7 security checkpoints. Present your Delegation Pass code.
