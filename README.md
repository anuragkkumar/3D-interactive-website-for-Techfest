# Techfest 2026 — 3D Celestial Observatory
### Indian Institute of Technology Bombay | 3D Interactive WebGL Submission (100 Points)

An interactive, award-winning 3D Celestial Solar Observatory designed for **Techfest 2026, IIT Bombay** (30th Edition). Built with Three.js (WebGL) without bloated templates or generic AI tropes, featuring procedural surface textures, multi-body gravitational mechanics, dynamic camera fly-to transitions, and full competition dossiers, schedules, and luminary speaker archives.

## 🪐 Key Features & Human Studio Architecture

### 1. Minimalist Editorial Viewport & Header Navigation
- Top Navigation Bar featuring interactive drawers:
  - **🌌 3D GALAXY:** Seamless panoramic overview.
  - **⚔️ ARENAS & RULES:** Comprehensive competition rulebooks (Robowars, Hackathon, Meshmerize, Drone Prix, AI Summit, Cozmo Clench).
  - **🗓️ 3-DAY SCHEDULE:** Full chronological itinerary across all three festival days at the IIT Bombay Powai campus.
  - **🎓 LUMINARIES:** Nobel Laureate and keynote speaker archives (Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam, Rakesh Sharma, Bjarne Stroustrup, Ada Yonath, Gordon Moore, Sophia the Humanoid).
  - **🎟️ DELEGATION PASS:** Campus ambassador accreditation, hostel lodging details, and verified entry code (`TECHFEST-IITB-2026`).

### 2. Interactive Planetary Fly-To Engine
- **Bottom Celestial Dock:** Instant smooth camera fly-to navigation across Techfest sectors:
  - `☀️ TECHFEST CORE` (The central powerhouse)
  - `⚔️ ROBOWARS` (Heavyweight combat arena)
  - `🧠 AI SUMMIT` (Neural computing track)
  - `📍 IIT BOMBAY` (Main Powai grounds & CA network)
  - `🚀 AEROSPACE` (ISRO pavilion & Drone Prix)
  - `🔬 EXHIBITIONS` (Humanoid robotics & global showcases)
  - `🎆 TECHNOHOLIX` (1,000-drone formation light symphony)
  - `🤖 MESHMERIZE` (Autonomous line maze solvers)
  - `💻 HACKATHON` (36-hour continuous build sprint)
- **Sector Telemetry HUD:** Real-time data readouts + **"Inspect Sector Dossier & Rules"** button.

### 3. Procedural In-Memory Planetary Textures
- **Earth:** Dynamic ocean depth shaders, organic continents, and swirling cloud layer.
- **Jupiter:** Layered horizontal fluid atmospheric bands with the Great Red Spot.
- **Saturn:** Double-sided ice particle rings with tilt (representing the 1,000-drone light show).
- **Mars:** Ochre rust canyons with polar ice caps.
- **Sun:** Multi-frequency solar turbulence with radiating corona.

### 4. Interactive 3D Orbit Controls
- **Click & Drag 360°:** Orbit around the entire solar system or around individual planets.
- **Scroll Zoom:** Zoom in/out smoothly with mouse wheel.
- **Cursor Parallax:** Natural perspective damping tracking cursor coordinates.
